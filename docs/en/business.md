# Business profile

## Identity

BQ Baños Químicos is a family-run portable sanitation business founded in 2017 in Villa Constitución, Santa Fe. Its initial expansion into the Vaca Muerta region centres on Rincón de los Sauces. The short name `BQ` may be used where the full display name has already been established.

The website serves an Argentinian audience and uses Argentinian Spanish (`es-AR`) for customer-facing content. Canonical technical documentation remains in British English.

## Positioning

The business presents itself through personalised service, reliable response, adherence to agreed schedules and practical sanitation solutions. Its principal audiences are construction, industrial and project-based organisations that need temporary on-site facilities.

Customer-facing copy should remain factual and specific. Claims about response times, availability, capacity or geographic coverage must be confirmed against current operations before publication.

## Service catalogue

The approved service categories are:

- portable toilet hire;
- delivery, transport and on-site installation;
- scheduled cleaning;
- pump-out and sanitisation;
- replenishment of hygiene consumables;
- preventive and corrective maintenance.

These categories describe the service scope without promising a particular frequency, capacity or location. Detailed packages and commercial conditions require operational approval before they become public copy.

## Operational principles

Service delivery is expected to prioritise hygiene, safety and operational efficiency. Personnel use appropriate protective equipment, receive task-specific training and follow procedures for cleaning, transport and responsible waste handling.

The website must not imply a certification, legal status or disposal process beyond evidence approved for publication.

## Publication boundaries

The following information is deliberately excluded from the canonical public profile until it has an explicit owner and approval:

- legal-person identifiers and registration records;
- private or unselected telephone, messaging and email channels;
- named clients or their logos;
- fleet totals, growth forecasts and guaranteed response times;
- exact coverage boundaries;
- certificates and compliance claims;
- prices, availability and contractual conditions.

The initial primary action may use telephone, WhatsApp, email or an external quotation channel. One authoritative destination must be selected before the action is implemented.
